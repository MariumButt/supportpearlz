## Alert codes
E-01 is inlet-water interruption. E-02 is low inlet pressure. E-03 is three short beeps with red filter light for sediment pre-filter saturation. E-04 is carbon service. E-05 is membrane performance warning. E-06 is leak sensor. E-07 is pump overload. E-08 is electrical/control fault.
